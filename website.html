Data Exploration of Tumblr OC Posts

Hello! This is a website skeleton for what will eventually be a data vizualization of most used tags and words associated with OC posts on tumblr.com. Right now, its quite empty because I want the scope of it to grow as I develop it.


[MOST COMMON CO-OCCURRING TAGS]
Bar chart of top 10, each entry with frequency percentage or # of posts

[MOST COMMON WORDS IN BIOS]
Scatter plot of frequent words sorted by types of posts. For example: lore, fanart of other ocs, concepts

[COLOR FREQUENCY MAP]
Chart of most used colors, both individual colors and color palettes. Maybe a seperate section for skin colors
